Where the numbers come from

EPIC3 → Markets: Paid radio, TV, digital, net media, and their fees update when EPIC3 changes. The 25 active 2026 markets produce 26 market orders because Denver has separate broadcast and digital orders. ESPN and weather each have one additional order.

Variables → billing: Total due ÷ total billing weeks = weekly payment. Each monthly amount = weekly payment × that month’s weeks. Campaign dates and duration are separate scheduling inputs. Amounts retain source precision and display rounded to cents.

Denver and Milwaukee: Denver’s partner allocation splits the broadcast buy; its fee comes from EPIC3 BW36. Milwaukee’s radio trade fee comes from EPIC3 AD6 and is included once. “Shared buy & trade” shows the detail.

Source differences: Linking corrects Milwaukee’s copied digital amount by $0.50 and ESPN’s copied total by $0.237. ESPN’s approved budget still comes from the source reconciliation tab. The full sheet preserves the historical invoice entries, source approval marks, and 150 pre-existing division errors in unfilled detail rows. Those entries do not indicate new approvals or payments.

Dates: The source mixes 2025 and 2026 campaign dates. Review flags identify conflicts; no dates were silently advanced. Selecting a Variables cell shows the separate Dates_NM reference, where available.
